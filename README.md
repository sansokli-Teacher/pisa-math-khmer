# គណិតវិទ្យាតាមបែប PISA — ការវាយតម្លៃលើកុំព្យូទ័រ

https://cba.khmermath.org — a computer-based practice test in Khmer, laid out
like the PISA delivery platform, from the teacher guide
«ក្របខណ្ឌគណិតវិទ្យារបស់ PISA ២០២២» (សាន សុខលី):

- three gold units (Grades 7, 8 and 9) and the practice tests ក–ឍ, the
  project's own PISA-style items;
- the seven OECD illustrative examples of the PISA 2022 mathematics
  framework, translated into Khmer (see NOTICE.md for source and licence).

None of this is an official OECD or PISA product. Difficulty levels are the
author's estimates, not an official calibration.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It needs no internet connection and no
installation.
