# គណិតវិទ្យាតាមបែប PISA — ការវាយតម្លៃលើកុំព្យូទ័រ

A computer-based practice test, in Khmer, laid out like the PISA delivery
platform. It holds the original units of the teacher guide
«ក្របខណ្ឌគណិតវិទ្យារបស់ PISA ២០២២» (សាន សុខលី): three gold units (Grades 7,
8 and 9) and the practice tests ក–ឍ.

These are the project's own PISA-style items. They are not official OECD or
PISA items, and their difficulty levels are the author's estimates, not an
official calibration.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It needs no internet connection and no
installation.

Built by `tools/build_public.py` from the private project; do not edit here.
